# HP BIOS/UEFI and Boot Menu Keys

A guide to entering the BIOS setup, Boot Menu, and System Diagnostics on Hewlett-Packard (HP and Compaq) computers.

## Primary Keys Summary

| Function | Primary Key | Alternative Keys |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Startup Menu** | Esc | - |
| **BIOS / UEFI Setup** | F10 | F2 |
| **Boot Menu** | F9 | - |
| **System Diagnostics** | F2 | - |

## HP Laptops (EliteBook, ProBook, Pavilion, Spectre, Envy, OMEN)

### Modern Systems (2015 - Present)
* **Startup Menu:** Press **Esc** repeatedly as soon as the power button is pressed.
* **BIOS / UEFI Setup:** Press **F10** directly or press **Esc** then **F10**.
* **Boot Menu:** Press **F9** directly or press **Esc** then **F9**.

### Legacy Systems (Pre-2015)
* **Older HP Pavilion / Envy:** Press **F10** or **F2**.
* **Legacy Compaq Presario / Armada:** Press **F10** when the cursor flashes in the top right corner during boot, or press **F2**.

## HP Desktops (EliteDesk, ProDesk, Pavilion Desktop, OMEN Desktop, Z Workstations)

### Modern Systems (2015 - Present)
* **BIOS / UEFI:** Press **F10** repeatedly at boot.
* **Boot Menu:** Press **F9** repeatedly at boot.

### Legacy Systems (Pre-2015)
* **Legacy Workstations & Business Desktops:** Press **F10**.
* **Older Pavilion / Compaq Desktops:** Press **F1** or **F10**.

## Additional HP Shortcuts
* **HP System Diagnostics:** Press **F2** repeatedly at boot.
* **System Recovery Partition:** Press **F11** repeatedly at boot.

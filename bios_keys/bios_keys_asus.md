# ASUS BIOS/UEFI and Boot Menu Keys

A guide to entering the BIOS setup and Boot Menu on ASUS motherboards, laptops, and desktop systems.

## Primary Keys Summary

| Function | Primary Key | Alternative Keys |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **BIOS / UEFI Setup** | F2 / Del | - |
| **Boot Menu** | F8 / Esc | - |

## ASUS Laptops (ZenBook, ROG, TUF, VivoBook, ProArt)

### Modern Systems (2015 - Present)
* **BIOS / UEFI Setup:** Press and hold **F2** before pressing the Power button, then release when the BIOS screen appears. Alternatively, tap **F2** repeatedly upon power-on.
* **Boot Menu:** Press and hold **Esc** before pressing the Power button, or tap **Esc** repeatedly at power-on.

### Legacy Systems (Pre-2015)
* **Older Laptops:** Press **F2** or **Del**.
* **Legacy Eee PC:** Press **F2** or **F9** (for recovery).

## ASUS Custom Motherboards & Desktop PCs (ROG, TUF Gaming, Prime, ProArt)

* **BIOS / UEFI Setup:** Press **Del** or **F2** repeatedly during POST (Power-On Self-Test).
* **Boot Menu:** Press **F8** repeatedly during POST.

## Additional ASUS Shortcuts
* **EZ Flash Utility (BIOS Update):** Accessible inside the BIOS by pressing **F7** (Advanced Mode) -> Tool -> ASUS EZ Flash Utility.

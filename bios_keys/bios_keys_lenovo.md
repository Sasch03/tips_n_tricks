# Lenovo BIOS/UEFI and Boot Menu Keys

A guide to entering the BIOS setup, Boot Menu, and Rescue System on Lenovo and IBM ThinkPad computers.

## Primary Keys Summary

| Function | Primary Key | Alternative Keys |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **BIOS / UEFI Setup** | F1 / F2 | Fn + F1 / Fn + F2 |
| **Boot Menu** | F12 | Fn + F12 |
| **NOVO Button** | Physical Button | Pin-hole Switch |

## ThinkPad Series (Laptops & Mobile Workstations)

### Modern ThinkPads (T, X, P, E, L series - 2018 - Present)
* **BIOS / UEFI Setup:** Press **F1** repeatedly during startup. If Function Lock is enabled, use **Fn + F1**.
* **Boot Menu:** Press **F12** repeatedly during startup (or **Fn + F12**).
* **Interrupt Startup Menu:** Press **Enter** at the Lenovo splash screen to pause boot and choose options.

### Legacy ThinkPads & IBM ThinkPads (Pre-2018)
* **Older ThinkPads:** Press **F1** or press the blue **ThinkVantage / Access IBM** button during boot.

## IdeaPad, Yoga, Legion, and Essential Laptops

### Modern Systems (2015 - Present)
* **BIOS / UEFI Setup:** Press **F2** (or **Fn + F2**) repeatedly at startup.
* **Boot Menu:** Press **F12** (or **Fn + F12**) repeatedly at startup.
* **NOVO Button:** Many IdeaPad/Yoga models feature a physical **NOVO button** (a small pinhole switch on the side/bottom). Press this button while the laptop is powered off to launch the Novo Menu (BIOS, Boot Menu, Recovery).

## Lenovo Desktops (ThinkCentre, IdeaCentre, Legion Desktop)

* **BIOS / UEFI Setup:** Press **F1** or **F2** repeatedly at startup.
* **Boot Menu:** Press **F12** repeatedly at startup.

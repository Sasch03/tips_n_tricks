# Dell BIOS/UEFI and Boot Menu Keys

A guide to entering the BIOS setup and Boot Menu on Dell desktop and laptop computers.

## Primary Keys Summary

| Function | Primary Key | Alternative Keys |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **BIOS / UEFI Setup** | F2 | Del |
| **Boot Menu** | F12 | - |

## Dell Laptops (Latitude, XPS, Inspiron, Precision, Alienware)

### Modern Systems (2015 - Present)
* **BIOS / UEFI:** Press **F2** repeatedly upon powering on when the Dell logo appears.
* **Boot Menu:** Press **F12** repeatedly upon powering on.

### Legacy Systems (Pre-2015)
* **Latitude (C, CP, CS series):** Press **Fn + F1** or **F2**.
* **Older Inspiron / XPS:** Press **F2**.
* **Very Old Models (1990s - early 2000s):** Press **Ctrl + Alt + Enter** or **Fn + Esc**.

## Dell Desktops (OptiPlex, XPS Desktop, Vostro, Inspiron Desktop, Precision Workstations)

### Modern Systems (2015 - Present)
* **BIOS / UEFI:** Press **F2** repeatedly at the Dell splash screen.
* **Boot Menu:** Press **F12** repeatedly at the Dell splash screen.

### Legacy Systems (Pre-2015)
* **OptiPlex / Dimension (Older models):** Press **F2** or **Del**.
* **Legacy Dimension Series:** Press **Ctrl + Alt + Enter** or **F2**.
* **Older Workstations:** Press **F2** or **F10** (for specific utility partitions).

## Additional Dell Shortcuts
* **Dell ePSA Diagnostics:** Press **F12** at startup and select "Diagnostics", or hold **Fn + Power Button** from an off state.
* **BIOS Recovery:** Hold **Ctrl + Esc** while plugging in the AC adapter from an off state.
